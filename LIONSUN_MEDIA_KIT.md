## 🎮 1. GAME OVERVIEW

### **Core Concept**
**Genre**: 2D Platformer / Adventure Puzzle  
**Style**: Hand-drawn, emotionally immersive world  
**Platform**: Cross-platform (iOS, Android, PC, Console-ready)  
**Theme**: Psychological liberation through gaming  

### **Main Characters**
- **🐭 Star Mouse** (Player Character): A brave, nimble guide representing hope and determination
- **🦁 Lionsun** (Central Figure): A majestic lion trapped in psychological barriers, symbolizing inner strength waiting to be unleashed

### **Gameplay Mechanics**
- **Memory Fragment Collection**: Gather pieces of Lionsun's past to unlock new abilities
- **Light-Based Puzzles**: Use illumination to solve environmental challenges
- **Boss Battles**: Confront manifestations of psychological barriers
- **Progressive Unlocking**: Each victory opens new mental landscapes

### **Narrative Journey**
Players guide Star Mouse through surreal, beautiful landscapes representing different aspects of the mind. Each level tells part of Lionsun's story, creating an emotional connection that goes beyond traditional gaming.
