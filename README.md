# 🌞 LionsunCoin (ShiRoKhorShid)

**LionsunCoin (ShiRoKhorShid)** is the exclusive digital currency powering the **Lionsun Gaming Platform**.  
It combines gaming, blockchain, and cultural identity into a single Play-to-Earn ecosystem.

---

## 🎯 Vision
- Enable gamers worldwide to **Play, Earn, and Trade**.  
- Provide a secure and efficient in-game currency for digital transactions.  
- Preserve cultural roots with the **ShiRoKhorShid** identity while staying globally relevant.

---

## 🕹️ Core Utility
- In-game purchases: skins, DLCs, upgrades, and premium content.  
- Rewards for completing missions, challenges, and active gameplay.  
- Peer-to-peer exchange of items between players.  
- Future extension to **NFTs and external payments**.

## 💡 Key Features
- **Blockchain integration** (Ethereum L2 / Polygon / Cosmos).  
- **Lionsun Wallet** for storing and transferring tokens.  
- **Cross-platform support**: PS5, Xbox, iOS, Android, Windows.  
- **Reward system**: collect *Sun Orbs* → convert to LionsunCoin.  
- **Community-driven allocation** for gamers and developers.  

---

## 📊 Tokenomics
- **Total Supply**: Fixed (e.g., 100M).  
- **Distribution**:
  - 40% → Player rewards (Play-to-Earn).  
  - 25% → Development & ecosystem.  
  - 20% → Team & early investors.  
  - 10% → Marketing & partnerships.  
  - 5% → Reserve (security & stability).
